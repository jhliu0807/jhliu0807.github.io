About

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Fudan University. My research focuses on personalized AI and recommender systems, with particular interests in LLM-based agents, interactive technologies, causal inference, and graph learning for efficient and trustworthy information access.

I have more than two years of industry research experience at ByteDance, Alibaba, and Tencent through talent programs. Several of my projects have been deployed in products serving more than 100 million daily active users.

I will join Zhongguancun Academy as an Assistant Professor in December 2026. Prospective PhD students are welcome to contact me, and I am also open to research collaborations. 我将于 2026 年 12 月加入中关村学院，担任助理教授。诚挚欢迎有意攻读博士学位的同学与我联系，也期待与各位同行开展合作。
