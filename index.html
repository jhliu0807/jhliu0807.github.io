About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Fudan University, where I study personalized AI and recommender systems. My research explores LLM-based agents, interactive technologies, causal inference, and graph learning for efficient and trustworthy information distribution.

I have spent more than two years conducting algorithm research at ByteDance, Alibaba, and Tencent through talent programs. Several projects have been fully deployed in products with over 100 million daily active users.

I will join Zhongguancun Academy as an Assistant Professor in December 2026. Prospective Ph.D. students are warmly encouraged to contact me, and I also welcome opportunities for research collaboration. (我将于2026年12月加入中关村学院，担任助理教授。诚挚欢迎有意攻读博士学位的同学与我联系，也期待与各位同行开展合作。)
