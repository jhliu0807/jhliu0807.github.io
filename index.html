About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Fudan University. Through industry talent programs, I have gained more than two years of research experience at ByteDance, Alibaba, and Tencent. Several of my projects have been deployed in products serving hundreds of millions of daily active users. My work spans large language models, intelligent agents, multimodal learning, and recommender systems. I have published 13 papers at CCF A-ranked venues, including 9 as first or co-first author, and 5 papers at CCF B-ranked venues, including 3 as first or co-first author.
